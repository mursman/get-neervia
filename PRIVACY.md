# Privacy Policy

_Effective 29 October 2026 · Neervia (Android and Windows), by Murscode.
The canonical copy of this policy lives at
[neervia.web.app/privacy](https://neervia.web.app/privacy)._

Neervia moves files directly between your own devices on your own local
network. It was built so that your files never pass through us — and this
policy explains, plainly, what that means.

## We have no servers and no accounts

Neervia has no sign-up, no user accounts, and no cloud component. We do not
operate servers that receive, store, or can see your files, messages, or
transfer history. Transfers travel directly between your devices over your
local network (Wi-Fi, hotspot, or Ethernet), protected by encrypted
connections (TLS) between devices you have paired using a verification code.

## What stays on your devices

The app stores the following only on your own devices, so it can work: a
self-generated device identity, the list of devices you have paired, your
transfer history and chat with each device, and your settings. You can
remove a paired device and its history at any time in the app. Uninstalling
the app deletes this data.

## Advertising (Android app)

The Android app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure
ads, Google may collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID,
general app activity, and diagnostic information, as described in
[Google's policy for partner sites and apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
A one-time in-app purchase ("Remove Ads") removes ads entirely. The Windows
app contains no ads and no advertising SDK.

## Crash reports and usage analytics (Android app)

The Android app uses Google's Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics.
If the app crashes, Crashlytics sends us a crash report — the stack trace,
the app version, and basic device state (model, system version, free
storage and memory) — so we can find and fix the fault. Analytics tells us,
in aggregate, how the app is used: how many people use it, which screens
they open, and whether features such as file transfer and remote control
get used. It never sees your files, your messages, or the names of your
devices. Both services use a per-install identifier created by Firebase;
how Google handles this data is set out in
[Firebase's privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).
We do not use this data for advertising and we never sell it. The Windows
app contains neither service.

If you choose to use the app's "Send diagnostics" feature, a diagnostic log
is sent to your own paired computer — never to us.

## Permissions, and why

- Local network access — to find and talk to your other devices. This is
  the core of the app.
- Bluetooth (optional, off by default) — only if you turn on Bluetooth
  discovery, or pair a Bluetooth gamepad for remote control. The app never
  asks for your location.
- Notifications — to let you accept or decline incoming transfers and see
  transfer progress.
- Photos, videos and files — only the items you pick to send, and the
  items you accept from your paired devices.
- Install packages — used only when you open an APK file you received and
  choose to install it; the Android system installer always shows its own
  confirmation.

## Children

Neervia is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children
under 13.
